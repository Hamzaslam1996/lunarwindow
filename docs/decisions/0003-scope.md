# ADR 0003 — Scope for the hackathon build

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-09-24

In: south-pole sites, Sun/Earth over local horizon, illumination + power models, DTE windows,
site × date comparison, assurance export, simple frontend.
Out (post-hackathon): relay-satellite comms, thermal model, descent-trajectory lighting,
rover traverses, north pole and non-polar sites (trivially supported by the geometry, but not
validated), and any commercial features.
